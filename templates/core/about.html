About the Evidence

See where the figures come from, how snapshots are kept, and what a gap in the data means.

Fixed Evidence Scope

CiteGrid is a focused evidence ledger designed to make development data citations reproducible. It is strictly bounded to three countries and three indicators across annual historical records from 2000 to the present.

Countries (3)
  • NGA Nigeria
  • GHA Ghana
  • KEN Kenya
Indicators (3)
  • Total EG.ELC.ACCS.ZS
  • Rural EG.ELC.ACCS.RU.ZS
  • Urban EG.ELC.ACCS.UR.ZS

Missing-Value Policy

Historical data frequently contains gaps. When a provider supplies no figure for an observation year, CiteGrid records and renders the value as “No data”. Missing values are never imputed, never substituted with zero (0.0%), and never connected across gaps with artificial line segments.

Retrieval Date vs. Observation Year

An observation year describes the historical calendar year the metric represents. The retrieval timestamp describes the exact point in time when CiteGrid fetched and validated the complete dataset from the provider.

Content-Addressed Immutability

Every published snapshot receives a canonical SHA-256 content hash computed over normalized observation records. Published snapshots and their values are strictly immutable.
